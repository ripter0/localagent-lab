About CleanSignal

Empowering communities to monitor and report environmental pollution.

Mission

CleanSignal is a citizen-powered platform that enables community members to report pollution incidents, track environmental issues, and hold authorities accountable. We believe that every voice matters when it comes to protecting our shared environment.

How It Works

  1. Report: Use our simple form to submit pollution observations with location, type, and severity.
  2. Verify: Reports are reviewed by community moderators and local authorities.
  3. Act: Verified reports trigger alerts to relevant agencies for response.
  4. Track: Follow the status of reports until resolution.

Our Values

  • Transparency: All reports are publicly visible (with privacy protections)
  • Community: Built by citizens, for citizens
  • Accountability: Help authorities respond faster to environmental issues
  • Innovation: Using technology to solve real-world problems
